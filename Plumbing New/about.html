About Us

Discover our team, our vision, and our commitment to keeping your student accommodation running smoothly.

Our Story

QuickFix Plumbing is a dedicated, student-centric plumbing support provider operating right here in Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa. From day one, our goal has been straightforward: to remove the stress from property maintenance and make plumbing support accessible to every student.

We launched after noticing how many university and college students face constant maintenance roadblocks—ranging from blocked drains and leaking taps to tricky geyser issues—without the budget for commercial plumbing rates. We created QuickFix Plumbing to bridge that gap with fair pricing.

Up until now, our growth has been driven by social media updates and great word-of-mouth recommendations. This platform marks our transition into a professional digital space, making it even easier for students across the city to connect with our team.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to deliver dependable, cost-effective plumbing support customized for the academic community. We believe a tight budget should never stand between a student and a safe, comfortable living environment.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to build Cape Town’s premier student plumbing support network, celebrated for fair rates, rapid service, and an authentic dedication to helping students maintain their peace of mind.

Who We Help

Our specialized services are tailored for:

  • Rosebank College students
  • University students throughout the Cape Town region
  • Off-campus and private digs tenants needing hands-on maintenance help
  • Student communes and shared housing groups requiring reliable plumbing upkeep

Our Team

We are a collection of maintenance enthusiasts who remember exactly what it’s like to balance assignment deadlines with household emergencies. We combine our trade expertise with clear, jargon-free communication so you always feel understood and supported.
