Our Services

Reliable, budget-friendly plumbing maintenance — exactly when you need it.

What We Offer

1. Emergency Leak Detection & Repair

We diagnose and resolve common pipe and pipe fixture problems quickly and affordably. Whether you have a hidden leak, a burst pipe, dripping taps, or low water pressure, our plumbers can help.

  • Hidden leak detection and pipe sealing
  • Burst pipe repairs and emergency patching
  • Dripping tap maintenance and washer replacement
  • Water pressure assessments and adjustments
  • Pipe inspection and health checks

Price range: From R150 depending on the issue.

2. Appliance Installation & Configuration

We install and properly connect the water-based appliances you need for your home or laundry room, ensuring everything runs smoothly from the start.

  • Washing machine water inlet and drain setup
  • Dishwasher connection and testing
  • Kitchen sink waste disposal unit installation
  • Water filtration systems and filtration tools
  • Drain hose extension and valve upgrades

Price range: From R100 per appliance install.

3. Bathroom Layout Formatting Assistance

Struggling with your bathroom refurbishment layout? We help homeowners arrange and install fixtures correctly according to local plumbing requirements.

  • Shower tray and cubicle alignment
  • Basin placement and waste trap configuration
  • Toilet waste outlet and cistern setup
  • Wall spacing and pipe depth adjustments
  • Inserting shower heads, mixers, and trim plates

Price range: From R80 per fixture.

4. Basic Geyser Setup

We help property owners and small commercial spaces establish a basic hot water presence with a simple, professional geyser setup.

  • Single-element or multi-valve geyser assembly
  • Inlet pipe and pressure control valve development
  • Drip tray and safety discharge line guidance
  • Thermostat and heating element configuration
  • Energy-efficient system layout

Price range: From R500 for a basic geyser.

5. Blocked Drain Clearing & Recovery

Lost full drainage capacity or worried about a backed-up system? We assist with clearing out and recovering domestic drainage lines.

  • Kitchen sink trap clearing and block removals
  • Sewer line rod inspection and configuration
  • Debris recovery attempts for blocked or slow-draining systems

Price range: From R120.
